ABOUT US

Built on two disciplines.
United by one standard.

Omnitegra Associates was founded on a simple belief: a firm can do everything — and still do it right. Too often, businesses are forced to choose between specialists who excel in one field and generalists who master none. We exist to close that gap.

Our founding team brings together the analytical rigour of a Chartered Accountant and the creative intelligence of a National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) design graduate. That combination — numbers and narrative, structure and style — defines how we approach every engagement.

Headquartered in Pune, we work with individuals, startups, small businesses and established enterprises. Whether the need is compliance, strategy, branding, product design or trade — we bring the same standard: integrity first.
